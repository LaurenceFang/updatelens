Professional judgment: no blocking visual problem remains. A fifth redesign is not warranted. The implementation preserves the accepted product structure and design system, with deliberate content/density adaptations for the real required flow.

1. **Color and surface:** the true-white canvas matches the accepted direction; rail and inspector use cool light neutrals, with forest controls and restrained amber association/coverage markers. No warm-tinted canvas or decorative card grid was introduced.
2. **Brand/navigation:** the quadrant lens mark, bold wordmark, small Review/Sources/Method navigation and active underline follow the concept. “Local service” intentionally clarifies runtime versus sample-data provenance.
3. **Primary hierarchy:** modest title/subtitle, compact left setup, open divider-separated findings and a strong evidence pane preserve the three-column hierarchy. The static heading no longer dominates the working viewport.
4. **Finding anatomy:** the pale green selected row, forest left rule, outlined association dot, strong title, concise explanation, project/locator metadata and quiet chevron preserve the concept's list motif. Actual eight grouped observations replace the concept's three illustrative rows.
5. **Controls and type:** consistent outlined selectors, forest toggles and small radii match the concept. Long skill/model labels now wrap within their own cells; critical metadata is 11–12px with 13px readable body/evidence. OS has a distinct label and separate CI description.
6. **Primary action:** Analyze remains visible in the persistent footer at 1280×720 and in collapsed mobile setup, alongside honest selection provenance and source coverage. This intentionally improves on the concept's static bottom placement for shorter screens.
7. **Evidence:** numbered official/local/association sections, code locators, source links and segmented user task states follow the concept. Actual named script/provider declarations are shown before generic file presence. Full source/evidence/action content remains accessible through disclosures and scrolling; no source statement was shortened into an invented fact.
8. **Responsive composition:** 390×844 stacks the workbench, collapses configuration controls and preserves the primary action. Root measured no horizontal body overflow and actually expanded setup and analyzed on mobile. The desktop source/evidence panes remain separate rather than being clipped off-screen.
9. **Intentional proportion/density differences:** the desktop rail is approximately 260px versus the concept's roughly 350px. This retains stable three columns on smaller laptop widths and gives real source/evidence text more room. Added OS selection, complete labels and multi-entry evidence create more content than the concept; project selection and task controls therefore require internal scrolling rather than fabricated short placeholder data or unreadable compression.
10. **Measured reachability:** root measured rail-controls `scrollHeight=775`, `clientHeight=569` at 1536×1024 and verified project checkboxes after an actual internal scroll while Analyze stayed fixed. Inspector `scrollHeight=1114`, `clientHeight=854`; actual wheel input exposed Pending at 822–857px and the verification note. Hidden-first-fold controls are a deliberate bounded-scroll layout choice, not unreachable UI.
